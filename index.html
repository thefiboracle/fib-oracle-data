Every reading is committed to a public repository the day it's taken. Past entries can't be edited without it showing in the history, which is the point of using git rather than a spreadsheet.

The hypothesis was registered at n=3, before there was enough data to fit anything to. Aligned means within 10 degrees of the north–south axis, so chance is 11.1%. Those definitions don't change.

Subject 001

Echo. Border Collie, turned one in May, lives on the Gold Coast. He has no idea any of this exists.

In 2013 researchers logged 1,893 defecations across 70 dogs and found they preferentially align north–south, with the alignment collapsing during geomagnetic disturbance. This project tests whether that holds for one dog, badly, in public.
